DOOT Astronomy e-Magazine

I co-founded DOOT, an astronomy e-magazine initiated at the Indian Institute of Astrophysics during the COVID-19 pandemic.

As a senior editor, I helped coordinate articles, interviews, and other contributions to make astronomy accessible to a wider audience.

Other Outreach Activities

Science Festivals & Public Astronomy Events

Regular participation in the Texas A&M Physics Festival, National Science Day celebrations at the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, and special astronomy outreach events, including solar eclipse observations and Zero Shadow Day.

School & College Outreach

Lectures and interactive sessions for school and college students on astronomy, cosmology, and fundamental physics, including talks at Markaz Garden, Caliph Life School, and PSMO College in Kerala.

Workshops & Educational Activities

Hands-on cosmology sessions at COSMOS-IIA, University of Mysore (2023), and a three-day lecture series on Big Bang cosmology (2020), along with other educational activities introducing students to modern astronomy.
