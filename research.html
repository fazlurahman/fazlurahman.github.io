Research

My research focuses on understanding astrophysical foregrounds and their impact on precision measurements of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB). I am particularly interested in connecting the statistical and morphological properties of Galactic emission with the requirements of component separation and cosmological inference.

Galactic Foregrounds for CMB Cosmology

Galactic emission from thermal dust, synchrotron radiation, and other astrophysical components is one of the major challenges for precision CMB measurements. I study the spatial, statistical, and spectral complexity of these foregrounds and how this complexity affects the recovery of cosmological signals.

Non-Gaussianity & Statistical Morphology

I develop and apply statistical tools to characterize non-Gaussian structure in astrophysical random fields. My work includes higher-order statistics, Minkowski functionals, and related morphological diagnostics applied to Galactic foreground maps and simulations.

These statistics provide information beyond the power spectrum and help identify characteristic structures, scale dependence, and departures from Gaussian random-field behavior.

Component Separation & Simons Observatory

I work on methods for separating Galactic and extragalactic foreground emission from the CMB, with particular interest in polarization and searches for faint cosmological signals.

As a member of the Simons Observatory collaboration, I am involved in foreground and map-analysis studies aimed at understanding the statistical properties of observed maps and improving the robustness of cosmological analyses.

ISM Structure, Filaments & MHD Simulations

A complementary part of my research is aimed at connecting observed Galactic foreground statistics with the underlying physics of the interstellar medium.

I am particularly interested in filamentary structure, magnetic-field geometry, turbulence, and magnetohydrodynamic simulations, and in understanding how these physical processes imprint non-Gaussian structure on polarized Galactic emission.
